Retrieval-Augmented Generation

DocuMind — Document Q&A with cited sources

A RAG app: upload a PDF, and the backend extracts and chunks its text, embeds each chunk with Gemini, and stores the vectors in MongoDB. Ask a question in the chat-style frontend, and it retrieves the most relevant chunks, sends only that context to an LLM, and returns an answer with the exact source passages it used — or admits it doesn't know, rather than guessing.

Applied AI · Evaluation & Research

Handwritten Bill Extraction & Model Evaluation

A pipeline that extracts structured data — vendor, invoice number, date, amount, GST details — from photos of handwritten Indian bills using vision-language models, then scores each model's accuracy field-by-field against manually verified ground truth. Gemini scored 84.6% overall accuracy vs. 72.1% for a free OpenRouter model, with the largest gaps on amount and currency — the fields costliest to get wrong. Verified end-to-end by pushing extracted expenses live into Zoho Books via its API.

PythonGemini & OpenRouter vision modelsrapidfuzz scoringZoho Books API (OAuth2)
