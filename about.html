Hello, I'm Anshida

I'm a Computer Science Engineering student who enjoys building things end to end — from REST APIs and databases to the interfaces people actually use. I've interned across an ERP-focused startup and a Django-based development team, and I ship personal projects for fun: a retrieval-augmented document Q&A app, an LLM accuracy-evaluation pipeline wired into Zoho Books, and a handful of full-stack products and hackathon builds.

Outside of coursework, I've taken part in Kerala's largest women's hackathon, Tink-Her-Hack 3.0 — where I built and shipped DocTrace, a lost-and-found platform, live in one weekend — and an overnight build sprint at Foss-Hack. I like the pressure of building something real, fast, with a team.
